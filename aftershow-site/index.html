The calm corner of the internet.

Social apps got loud. Aftershow is built the other way: your history first, your friends second, nothing shouting for attention.

Most social apps

  • An algorithm decides what you see
  • Ads between your friends’ posts
  • Follower counts as a scoreboard
  • Infinite scroll, no ending

Aftershow

  • Chronological. Newest first, always
  • No ads in the feed, ever
  • Your history is the identity, not your audience size
  • Private mode is one switch

Questions people ask first.

Do I need a ticket to log a show?

No. Aftershow never asks for proof. Pick an artist, a venue and a best-guess date and it’s in your archive.

Can I move my history from Concert Archives?

Yes. Export your list from Concert Archives (it emails you a spreadsheet), save it as CSV and drop it into Aftershow’s importer. You check the column mapping before anything is added.

Who can see my shows?

By default, anyone can see what you log, Letterboxd-style. One switch in Settings makes your profile private so only people who follow you can see it.

Who owns the photos I upload?

You do. You grant Aftershow a license to show them in the app. The details are in the Photo & UGC License.

Does it work on my phone?

Yes. It’s a website that adapts to phones, tablets and desktops, with a tab bar on small screens and a sidebar on large ones.

What does it cost?

Nothing while we build it.

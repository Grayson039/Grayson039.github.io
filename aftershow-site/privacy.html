Privacy policy.

Last updated November 5, 2026. This is a plain-English template, not legal advice. Have a licensed attorney review it before public launch.

1. The short version

Aftershow is a place to log concerts. We collect what we need to run your account and show your shows, we don’t sell it, we don’t run ads, and we don’t use third-party analytics or tracking cookies.

2. What we collect

  • Account: email address, username and a password (stored hashed by our authentication provider; we never see it).
  • What you add: shows, ratings, notes, setlists, photos, comments, likes, follows, your profile picture and your four favourites.
  • Technical data: your IP address and browser details appear in standard server logs at our hosting and database providers.
  • On your device: your theme choice and, in demo mode, sample data are kept in your browser’s local storage. These are strictly necessary for the site to work and are never sent to us.

3. How we use it

  • To run the service: sign you in, show your archive, feed, stats and activity.
  • To keep it safe: prevent abuse, handle reports and copyright notices.
  • To contact you about your account (for example email confirmation or a password reset). We don’t send marketing email unless you opt in.

4. Who can see your content

Logs are visible to other members by default. Turn on private mode in Settings to limit your logs to you and the people who follow you. Photos you add are covered by the Photo & UGC License.

5. Who we share data with

Only the service providers that run Aftershow, acting on our instructions:

  • Supabase: database, authentication and photo storage.
  • Cloudflare: hosting and delivery of the website.
  • Google Fonts: serves the typefaces. Your browser contacts Google to load them, which shares your IP address with Google. We plan to host the fonts ourselves.

We don’t sell personal data. We may disclose information if the law requires it.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Access and export: download your archive as a CSV in Settings.
  • Edit or delete content: change or remove any log, photo or comment at any time.
  • Delete your account: email us at the address below and we will delete your account and content within 30 days.
  • Depending on where you live (for example the EU, UK or California) you may have additional rights to access, correct, delete, port or object to the use of your data. Contact us to use them.

7. How long we keep it

While your account is open. When you delete it, we remove your profile and content, apart from encrypted backups that expire on a rolling schedule and anything we must keep by law.

8. Children

Aftershow is for people 13 and older. We don’t knowingly collect data from children under 13. If you believe a child has joined, contact us and we will delete the account.

9. Security

Connections are encrypted, passwords are hashed and database access is restricted per user. No system is perfectly secure, so please use a strong, unique password.

10. Changes and contact

If we change this policy in a meaningful way we will tell members before it takes effect.
